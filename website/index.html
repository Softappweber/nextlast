FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can I really take calls on my PC?

Yes. Open SoloDS on your PC, go online, share your link. When someone calls, your PC rings with full mic and speaker support. Your phone can stay in your pocket.

Can I send files from my phone to my PC?

Yes. Open SoloDS on both devices with the same link. They'll see each other. Send any file — the other device receives it directly, in original quality.

Do I need a Google account or phone number?

No. Never. SoloDS works without phone numbers, Google logins, or OTPs. That's the whole point.

What does the free 5-minute demo include?

Everything. Voice, video, files, screen share — all limited to 5 minutes per session. Enough to know if you love it.

Is it really private?

Yes. Every call is encrypted end-to-end, device to device. Nothing goes through our servers. We can't see, hear, or record anything.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Works in any browser. For paid plans, we also give you a personal app link you can add to your home screen — feels like a native app.

Why is it so cheap?

Because we don't run expensive servers. Your device does the heavy lifting. That's why we can charge ₹99 while others charge ₹1,100.
