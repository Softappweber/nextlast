What is this?

A fallback connection method for when link sharing can't reach your partner. Instead of clicking a link, you and your partner exchange short codes that carry the connection details.

When to use it

Use this when link mode fails: partner offline, corporate firewall blocking WebRTC, or you simply prefer not to expose a public handle. Works between any two browsers, anywhere.

How it works

  1. You generate a code and send it to your partner (any channel — email, SMS, paste site).
  2. Partner pastes it here, generates a reply code, sends it back to you.
  3. You paste the reply code, and the connection opens.

Two messages exchanged. No server ever sees your chat.
